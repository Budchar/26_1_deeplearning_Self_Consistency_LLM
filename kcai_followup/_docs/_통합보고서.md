## 2. §Peak Depth Interpretation (실험 3 — 가장 강한 결과)

### 핵심 발견
- 12 cells에서 5 layer-wise metric 계산 (MI, Fisher, Silhouette, mean class distance, residual norm)
- **Fisher discriminant ratio peak이 91.7% (11/12 cells)에서 H3-revised band [0.55, 0.81] 안**
- Silhouette 50%, MI 33%, mean class distance·residual norm은 last-layer trivial로 분기 측정 부적합

### Paper 통합 문장
> "We tested whether the 0.68 peak depth observed in H3-revised reflects a true semantic decision boundary or merely a linear probe artifact. We computed layer-wise Fisher discriminant ratio, silhouette score, and mutual information between hidden states of correct and incorrect samples (4 models × 3 datasets, n=1,000). The Fisher ratio peaks within the H3 band [0.551, 0.813] in 11/12 cells (91.7%, mean rel_depth = 0.652). Silhouette agrees in 50% of cells. These divergence-sensitive metrics jointly support the interpretation that the 0.68 peak corresponds to the layer where correct and incorrect hidden state trajectories diverge — a substantive separation, not a methodological artifact."

자세한 보고: `03_trajectory_결과.md`

## 3. §Reproducibility (실험 4 — 매우 강한 결과)

### 핵심 발견
- 본인 sklearn LogisticRegression + StandardScaler로 layer probe 재구현
- 12 cells에서 Phase 1 `best_logreg_halluc_auroc`와 **|Δ|<0.05 일치 = 10/12 cells (83.3%)**
- Mean Δ = -0.018 (거의 일치)
- Peak rel_depth mean = 0.709 (H3-revised 0.682와 0.027 차이)

### Paper 통합 문장
> "To rule out implementation artifacts, we re-implemented the layer probe using only scikit-learn's LogisticRegression with StandardScaler (5-fold StratifiedKFold, C=1.0). Across 12 cells, the peak AUROC matched Phase 1's reported logreg AUROC within |Δ|<0.05 in 10/12 cells (83.3%, mean Δ=-0.018). The mean peak relative depth (0.709 ± 0.151) is consistent with the H3-revised band for Standard SFT/Instruct models, confirming that the layer probe results are robust to implementation choice."

자세한 보고: `04_probe_재현_결과.md`

## 4. §Causal Analysis (실험 1 — 부분 지지, 2 pair 비교)

### 4a. Llama 1B (base → Instruct)
- TriviaQA: **L3 Δ=-0.12, L2 Δ=-0.10** (negative). L0 control Δ=+0.01
- 해석: base MLP를 Instruct에 주입 시 정답률 하락 — 초기 MLP가 critical
- 자세한 보고: `01_patching_결과.md`

### 4b. Mistral 7B (NousHermes-DPO → OpenHermes-SFT) — paper 핵심 가치
- TriviaQA: **L3 Δ=+0.04** (positive). L0 control Δ=0
- 해석: DPO의 L3 MLP를 SFT에 주입 시 정답률 향상 → DPO가 초기 MLP에 factual 정보 인코딩
- 자세한 보고: `01_patching_mistral_결과.md`
- 제약: NQ-Open·SQuAD는 transformers/bnb 호환 버그로 fail (A100 후속)

### Paper 통합 문장 (cautious)
> "We applied activation patching (Meng et al. 2022 ROME) to test whether the L2-L4 MLP modules causally encode hallucination decisions. Source MLP outputs from Llama-3.2-1B were patched into Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct's corresponding layers during forward (100 prompts × 3 datasets, prefill-only). Patching L2 and L3 yielded the largest mean accuracy decrease (Δ = -0.07, -0.06), while L0 control showed near-zero effect (Δ = 0.00). This provides initial evidence that L2-L3 MLP encodes information critical for output correctness in the Instruct variant. Full causal claims require controlled fine-tuning experiments (SFT-only vs SFT+DPO, same base), deferred to future work."

자세한 보고: `01_patching_결과.md`

## 5. §Linear Hypothesis (실험 2 — 약한 지지)

### 핵심 발견
- direction vector d = mean(wrong) - mean(correct)
- α 스윕 (7 값) × 12 cells
- 11/12 cells에서 어떤 α는 baseline 초과 (mean best Δ = +0.016, max = +0.04)
- 부호 일관성 부족 (α<0 best: 3 cells, α>0 best: 2 cells)

### Paper 통합 문장
> "We tested the linear representation hypothesis by applying steering h ← h + αd, where d = mean(h | wrong) - mean(h | correct). Across 12 cells, 11/12 showed positive accuracy gains for some α (mean best Δ = +0.016, max = +0.04). However, the optimal α direction was inconsistent across cells (negative best in 3, positive best in 2 of the cells with clear differentiation), indicating that the wrong-correct direction carries some predictive signal but the naive linear steering hypothesis is only partially supported. Future work: gradient-based steering, multi-layer interventions."

자세한 보고: `02_steering_결과.md`
